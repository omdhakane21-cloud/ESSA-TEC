About Our Association

Fostering innovation, engineering integrity, and collaborative learning at Terna College of Engineering.

Our Mission

To cultivate an intellectually stimulating environment where students transition theoretical circuit theories into real-world applications including IoT, VLSI, Embedded Systems, and Robotics.

Our Vision

To be acknowledged as the benchmark student collegiate chapter for electronics engineering, delivering world-class technical workshops, corporate internships, and research papers.
